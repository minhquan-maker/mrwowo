For heads of trade marketing & supply chain

Short-dated stock still turns into cash — without breaking price or losing track of a lot.

mrwowo clears short-dated stock, protects price, and keeps a record for every lot. You set the rules once; it proposes an action for each lot, sells through many channels, and writes every order back to one lot record.
