# 3-minute demo script — mrwowo (Phase 0)

**Audience:** head of trade marketing or supply chain at an FMCG brand owner or distributor.
**Goal of the meeting:** not to sell software — to learn *whether they would pay for this recovery, and how*.

> Say up front: "Every number here is sample data; the brands are fictional."
> Vietnamese version: [`vi/KICH-BAN-DEMO.md`](vi/KICH-BAN-DEMO.md). Switch the UI with **EN / VI** in the top bar.

## Prepare (30 seconds, before the meeting)

1. Open `app.html#/lo-hang`, then ⋮ → **Reset sample data**.
2. Pick the language your interviewee prefers (**EN** / **VI**, top right).
3. Operator: **Nguyễn Thu Hà — Head of Trade Marketing**.
4. Optional: open **3-minute demo script** in the sidebar for on-screen prompts.
5. Short on time: ⋮ → **Load pre-approved scenario** (8 approved, 1 rejected).

| Time | Screen | Do | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Lot board | Point at card colours, open a red card | "These are 15 dry lots from one distributor. Colour is days left. Every lot has a proposal and a reason — this green tea just crossed 30 days and the contract allows a factory return, so the proposal is *return to supplier*." |
| 0:30 | Lot board | Press ▶ on the timeline | "Let time pass. Prices step down… and **stop at the floor** you set. A lot that reaches 30 days **pulls itself** from every channel — nobody has to remember." |

| 2:35 | Lot report | Point at the big number, open a lot record, press **Finance summary CSV** | "This is the number finance cares about: **net recovery** — after channel fees, freight and handling — against liquidation or disposal. Per lot: what sold, at what price, where the rest went, who approved and when." |
| 3:00 | — | Stop | **"Would you pay for this?"** |

## Follow-up questions (pick 3–5)

**Pain**
- How many lots / how much stock value reach 30–60 days a month?
- How is it handled today: jobbers, disposal, factory returns, staff sales? Who decides?
- The last time short-dated stock was dumped on the market, what happened?

**Value & buying**
- Which number would make you use this: net recovery, floor protection, or the audit trail?
- Whose budget is it — trade marketing, supply chain or finance?
- If you paid, which model: a monthly fee, a share of the *extra* recovery, or a fee per lot?

**Risk & conditions**
- Which channels are completely off-limits? Which regions must be excluded?
- Is hiding the brand enough?
- To pilot one real lot, what data would we need and who has to agree?

## Never say

- "First in Vietnam", "the only…".
- "Up to 70% off" as a fact — 70% is only an example of a cap an owner might set.
- Momo, Shopee, TikTok Shop… as if they were partners. Say "a partner app (illustrative)".
- CO2e figures without the word "estimate".
